Artisan Lineage

A Commitment to Cold Pressing

Harvestvora was established with a singular, unwavering mission: to rescue the fading art of manual stone olive pressing and introduce it to luxury culinary design. In a world increasingly saturated by quick synthetic vegetable oils and mass-processed factory dressings, we dedicate our cellar to organic olive groves and raw mineral composts. We believe gourmet oil is not simply an ingredient; it is a structural representation of physical ease, tactile beauty, and press master craft.

Each olive batch is harvested individually from sustainable estate trees in Switzerland. Once received at our New York showroom, the fruit is branch-shaken, stone-crushed, and centrifuge-separated entirely by hand. Organic wax seals use beeswax and mineral dyes instead of cheap plastic caps, protecting your storage shelf and oil freshness. It is this minute attention to microscopic details that allows our orchard masters to sustain a Lifetime Restoration Guarantee.

Our Three Harvest Pillars

01. Hand Harvester

We refine our fruit harvests three times in clean water to isolate bird peck spots.

02. Granite Mills

Press operators grind whole olives manually to prevent high seed temperatures.

03. Acidity Testing

We measure the free fatty acid ratios, keeping them below the extra-virgin threshold.

The Grove Future

As Harvestvora looks to the upcoming decade, we remain anchored in our slow-craft principles. We maintain a small cellar of certified weavers, limiting our global output to a few hundred oil barrels per year. This exclusivity ensures that every client registered receives the undivided focus and care of our press masters. Harvestvora is a lifetime commitment to cold presses, hand-shaken boughs, and heritage agricultural preservation.
